  • .BIN / PS2 ELF (PlayStation) PS2 MIPS ELF & PS3 SELF PPU analyzedSLUS_210.83, SLUS_214.09 (PS2) and EBOOT.BIN (PS3 HP 1-4, HP 5-7, Dimensions) analyzed.
  • .xex / .xbe (Xbox & Xbox 360) XBEH & XEX2 containers analyzedLSW1/LSW2 default.xbe, LSW2 default.xex (360_exlist), and HP 1-4/5-7 Default.xex decrypted.
  • .exe (Windows PE32 & PE32+) x86 & AMD64 binaries analyzedLEGOSTA2.EXE (2006), LEGOBatman2.exe (2012), and harry1.exe/harry2.exe (2024) analyzed.
  • .gsc Universal (All TT Engine Generations 2005–2024; .nux/.nus in LSW1 Xbox, .nx2 in LSW2 Xbox)Compiled static scene geometry, room meshes, material graphs, portal bounds, and camera splines across Classic Nu2 (.nup/.nux/.nx2) and Next-Gen NXG/DX11 (_nxg.gsc, _dx11.gsc).
  • .ghg Universal (All TT Engine Generations 2005–2024; .hgx in LSW1 Xbox, .hx2 in LSW2 Xbox, .hgp in LSW1 PC/GCN)Skeletal character, minifigure, creature, and vehicle mesh container storing bone hierarchies, inverse bind matrices, skinning weights, LODs, and embedded or referenced textures.
  • .tex Era 3–4 (2008–2024 Console & PC Titles; inline in .ghg/.gsc in Era 1–2)Standalone DirectDraw Surface (DDS) textures (.tex) and multi-texture dictionary containers (.nxg_textures / .dx11_textures) storing DXT1/DXT5/BC1–BC7 mipmapped surfaces.
  • .tsh Era 3–4 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions)NuTextureSheet and texture sharing container (4CC.TXSH / TSHR / RHST) mapping FNV1UPPER hashes and UV sub-rectangles to shared minifigure decals and texture atlases.
  • .an3 Universal (.ani in LSW1; .an3 in LSW2–Dimensions; .ca3 on LSW2 X360; .anm in HP/Batman 2)Compressed keyframe skeletal animation tracks storing bone rotation quaternions, translation vectors, and scale curves over time.
  • .bsa Era 1–2 Specific (LSW1 & LSW2: 679 .bsa in LSW1, 1,999 .bsa + 184 .anl in LSW2)Early Nu2 morph-target / blend-shape animation (.bsa) paired 1-to-1 with skeletal clips (.ani/.an3) and animation list manifests (.anl).
  • .cd Era 3–4 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions; preceded by CHARSTXT.TXT in Era 1–2)Reflected EdFile serializer container defining modular minifigure parts, meshes (.ghg), textures (.tex), physics (.cpd), and animation sets (.as).
  • .cpd Era 3–4 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions)Reflected EdFile serializer container defining character collision capsules, mass distribution, and ragdoll physics constraints.
  • .as Era 3–4 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions)Reflected EdFile state-machine container binding character actions, locomotion states, combos, and spells to animation clips (.an3/.anm) and sound triggers.
  • .apj Era 3–4 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions: 2,520 files in Dimensions)Reflected EdFile animation project linking multiple animation sets (.as), rig layers, and additive blend trees.
  • .led Era 3–4 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions: 2,616 files in Dimensions)Reflected EdFile level entity placement container defining spawners, interactive LEGO builds, triggers, and object instances.
  • .ter Universal Across Era 1–4 (LSW1, LSW2, HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions; .ctr on LSW2 X360)Spatial octree collision mesh and surface material flag database used for ground raycasting, footstep audio material lookup, and physics collision.
  • .rtl Universal Across Era 1–4 (LSW1, LSW2, HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions)Per-room runtime lighting table defining point lights, directional suns, ambient spherical harmonics, and shadow caster flags.
  • .ai2 Universal (.ai2 in LSW1, LSW2, HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2; .aib in Batman 2 & Dimensions)AI navigation graph and pathfinding mesh nodes controlling NPC movement, companion following, and enemy patrol bounds.
  • .for Era 1–2 Specific (LSW1 & LSW2)Early Star Wars level interaction formats defining Force-manipulable object rules (.for), physics constraints (.cns), and LEGO build/burst assembly scripts (.bur).
  • .scp Era 1–3 (LSW1, LSW2, Indy, Batman 1, HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2)Hierarchical state-machine script controlling NPC behaviors, boss phases, and level triggers using State / Conditions / Actions blocks.
  • .sf Era 3–4 (HP 1–4: 677, HP 5–7: 4,296, Batman 2: 5,274, Dimensions: 222)Imperative event and puzzle scripting language used extensively in Creator/Builder/Gotham NXG titles before the Lua 5.1 transition.
  • .lua Era 4–5 (LEGO Dimensions: 9,858 files, LEGO Worlds, Skywalker Saga)Compiled Lua 5.1 bytecode (and plaintext Lua scripts) replacing legacy .scp/.sf state machines for gameplay, UI, and portal logic in Era 4+ titles.
  • .cu2 Universal (.cut in LSW1; .cu2 in LSW2–Batman 2; .cc2 on LSW2 X360; .cu3 in Dimensions)In-engine cinematic timeline synchronizing camera cuts, character skeletal/facial animations, lighting changes, and audio tracks.
  • .giz Era 3–4 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions: 2,520 files in Dimensions)Interactive level gizmo container storing GizObstacle, GizBuildit, GizForce, Blowup, GizmoPickup, Lever, ZipUp, HatMachine, Plug, Tube, and Turret records.
  • .git Era 3–4 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions)Structured ASCII block configuring per-sector camera framing, split-screen merge thresholds, fog, bloom, and post-processing.
  • .dno Era 3–4 (HP 1–4: 6,858, HP 5–7: 5,204, Batman 2: 3,240, Dimensions: 17,672)PinnerDynamicsObj physics, collision hull, joint constraint, convex mesh, and KD-terrain descriptor container for dynamic and breakable LEGO world objects.
  • .pak Universal (.fpk RFPK in LSW2; .pak/.pac 0x1234567A in HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions)Flat sub-archive bundling small related files (textures, AI scripts, animation caches) with a 20-byte entry table and additive 0x12345678 filename checksums.
  • .cbx Era 3–4 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions: 9,899 .cbx and 5,976 .cbs in Dimensions)Proprietary 'ChatterBoX' (!B0X) EA MicroTalk-variant compressed speech/SFX audio container (.cbx, 0x1B0-sample frames) and companion phoneme/lip-sync curves (.cbs).
  • .res Era 3–4 (Batman 2: 2,340 files, Dimensions: 9,854 files)Per-level and per-character resource manifest enumerating dependent meshes, textures, animations, and sound banks for streaming.
  • .sub Era 3c–5 (Batman 2: 2,408 files, Dimensions: 15,826 files)Time-coded localized subtitle and phoneme trigger stream introduced when TT Games transitioned from mimed grunts to full voice acting in LEGO Batman 2 (2012).
  • .pc_shaders Universal (Era 2 .vpo/.fpo on PS3; Era 3–4 .pc_shaders/.ps3_shaders; Remaster shaderblob_pc.blob)Precompiled platform GPU vertex and pixel/fragment shader libraries for Direct3D 9, Direct3D 11, Xbox 360 Xenos, and PS3 RSX.
  • .msf / .xma / .ogg / .vag Universal (Platform-Specific Codecs per Console/PC Target)Streaming music, ambience, and dialogue audio containers tailored to each target platform's hardware decoder.
  • .cfg Universal (All TT Engine Generations 2005–2024)Plaintext audio mix bus configurations (.cfg) and binary/scripted sound event trigger maps (.sfx).
  • .ptl Universal (LSW1 2005 through LEGO Dimensions 2015)Particle emitter system descriptors (.ptl) and keyframe parameter curves (.par) for sparks, magic spells, fire, smoke, and stud bursts.
  • .spl Universal (LSW1 2005 through LEGO Dimensions 2015)3D Bezier/Catmull-Rom spline curves (EDSPLINE v. 03) used for camera rails, coaster tracks, vehicle paths, and stud trails.
  • .ft2 Universal (.fnt in LSW1; .ft2 in LSW2–Dimensions)Bitmap font glyph metric table and kerning dictionary paired with controller button icon atlases.
  • .gip Era 3–4 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions)Menu layout pages (.gip), widget indices (.gix), and shared UI element libraries (.gil) inside framework-data/ and gui/.
  • .fmv Universal (Platform-Specific Video Containers)Pre-rendered video streams used for publisher/developer logos, attract mode reels, and pre-rendered cutscenes.
  • .txt Universal (All TT Engine Generations 2005–2024)Master game configuration scripts (GAME.TXT, LEVELS.TXT, CHARS.TXT, COLLECTION.TXT, 360_exlist, FILETABLE.TXT) and multilingual localization tables (text.csv).
  • .bin / .por / .ats LEGO Dimensions Exclusive (2015)180-byte NXP NTAG213 NFC Toy Tag dumps (.bin) encrypted with TEA (0xAA55 seed) alongside in-archive portal (.por) and ability/tag script (.ats) files.
  • .lxfml LEGO Worlds (2017) & Brick-Building TitlesLEGO Digital Designer (LDD) XML brick assembly blueprints (.lxfml), procedural voxel terrain chunks, and brick stud connectivity graphs.
