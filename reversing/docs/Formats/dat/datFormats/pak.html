.pak / .pac / .fpk (Flat Resource Sub-Archive)

Category: Archives & Containers · Scope: Universal (.fpk RFPK in LSW2; .pak/.pac 0x1234567A in HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions)

Format Specification · TT Games Engine

Flat sub-archive bundling small related files (textures, AI scripts, animation caches) with a 20-byte entry table and additive 0x12345678 filename checksums.

Binary Structure (0x1234567A / 0x12345678)

Verified directly against ICONS_TEX.PAK (2,361,498 B, 136 files) in LEGO Batman 2 and ai.pak / _an3_ps4.pak in LEGO Harry Potter:

OffsetSizeTypeFieldDescription
0x004UInt32VersionMagic0x1234567A (LE) or 0x7A563412 (BE).
0x044UInt32FileCountNumber of packed sub-files (N).
0x084UInt32TotalSizeTotal archive size in bytes.
0x0C20*NStruct[]EntriesFive uint32 fields per entry: NameOffset (relative to string table), DataOffset, DataSize, Zero, and NameChecksum.

Additive Checksum Invariant: NameChecksum = 0x12345678 + sum((byte)toupper(c)) over all characters in the entry filename. In LSW2 (2006), the predecessor format is .fpk (137 files on Xbox, 141 on Xbox 360) with magic RFPK.
