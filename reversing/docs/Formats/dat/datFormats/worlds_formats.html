Overview

Uniquely among TT Games titles, LEGO Worlds (2017) replaces pre-modeled static .gsc levels with a real-time procedural voxel terrain generator and allows importing/exporting standard LEGO Digital Designer (.lxfml / .lxf) brick blueprints alongside .CC40TAD (TOC v-10/-11) archives.
