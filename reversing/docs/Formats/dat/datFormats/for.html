Overview

Specific to the early LEGO Star Wars engine before the unified .giz / .led / .dno system:

  • .for: 86 files in LSW1, 27 in LSW2 — defines objects interactive with Jedi/Sith Force powers.
  • .cns: 72 files in LSW1, 20 in LSW2 — mechanical and spatial constraints.
  • .bur: 52 files in LSW2 — burst/breakable multi-brick object descriptors.
