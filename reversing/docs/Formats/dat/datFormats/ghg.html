Overview & Platform Variants

.ghg (Game Hierarchical Geometry) defines articulated characters, minifigures, creatures, and multi-part props:

  • LSW1 (2005): .ghg on PS2 (RNC\x02 compressed), .hgx on Original Xbox (uncompressed NU20 v0x25 with inline DXT .dds textures and C:\Perforce\LSW\... paths), and .hgp on PC/GameCube.
  • LSW2 (2006): .ghg on PS2/Xbox 360/PC and .hx2 on Original Xbox (accompanied by debug .hx2.htm Perforce export logs on the Xbox retail disc).
  • NXG & DX11 (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2, Dimensions): Platform-suffixed _nxg.ghg / _dx11.ghg combining an inner NU20 hierarchy (HGOL / LOGH layer lists, joints, locators) with GPU vertex/index streams.

Internal Chunks

  • NTBL / LBTN: Null-terminated string table holding joint names (head, neck, torso, arm_L, hand_L, hips, leg_L) and material identifiers.
  • TST0 / 0TST: Texture set table (inline textures in Era 1–2; external .tex / .nxg_textures references in Era 3–4).
  • MSH0 / 0HSM: Skinned mesh primitives and bone weight palette indices.
