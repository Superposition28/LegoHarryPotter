.LED (EdFile Level Editor & Entity Placement Graphs)

Dissected Across All 10 NXG PS3 Builds: Indy 2 (513) through Hobbit (6,926) and Dimensions (6,764).

LEGO Harry Potter: Years 1–4, Years 5–7 & 2024 Collection · EdFile Reflection · Era 3a–4 (Indy 2 through Dimensions)

Level scene graph serialized with EdFile (up to 6,926 files in Hobbit), storing placed instances, spawns, camera rails, gizmo triggers, and room state.

Extension
.led
Format
Unwrapped EdFile (StreamInfo v17..v30 at offset 0x00 + TypeList + ClassList + OLST)

.led is the single largest EdFile asset type in Era 3–4 games, tying 3D static geometry (.gsc) to gameplay entities, switches (.giz), ScriptFu/SCP logic (.sf / .scp), and cutscenes.

Cross-Format Relationships & Dependencies

Referenced By (Upstream Containers / Loaders)

  • .gsc / Area Loaders — Mounted per room folder alongside _nxg.gsc.

References (Downstream Assets / Sub-Formats)

  • .cd / .ghg — Places enemy/NPC/vehicle spawners.
  • .giz / .dno / .sf / .scp — Connects interactive bricks, physics objects, and triggers.

Filtering, Edge Cases & Unresolved Fields

  • Keeping StreamInfo unwrapped at 0x00 allows fast header peeking even when .res wraps everything else in .CC4HSER.
