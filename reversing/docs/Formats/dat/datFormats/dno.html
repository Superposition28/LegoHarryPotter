Overview & 12-Section Structure (PinnerDynamicsObj.cs)

Verified against TTGamesExplorerRebirthLib/Formats/PinnerDynamicsObj.cs: .dno stands for Pinner Dynamics Object (named after TT Games' internal Pinner physics/dynamics subsystem). Version 20 files begin with the 10-byte ASCII magic SERIALISED + uint32 Version (20) followed by 12 sequential sections:

  1. PinnerString (string table)
  2. PinnerMatrix (4×4 transform matrices)
  3. PinnerEntity (dynamic rigid-body entities)
  4. PinnerMesh (collision triangle meshes)
  5. PinnerJoint (hinge/ball/slider constraints)
  6. PinnerGeom (primitive collision shapes)
  7. PinnerSpline (path constraints)
  8. PinnerController (kinematic controllers)
  9. PinnerTileMap (grid collision tiles)
  10. PinnerConvexMesh (convex hull polytopes)
  11. KdTerrainDescriptor (KD-tree spatial terrain)
  12. Secondary PinnerMesh section
