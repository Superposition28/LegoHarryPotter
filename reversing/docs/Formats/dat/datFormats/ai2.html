Overview

.ai2 defines navigation polygons and link edges from LSW1 (120 files) through Batman 2 (85 files). In Batman 2 (284 .aib files) and LEGO Dimensions (856 .aib files), AI navmeshes transition to the updated .aib (AI Binary) format.
