Overview

In Era 1–2 (LSW1, LSW2), level and character textures are embedded directly inside .gsc/.nux/.nx2 and .ghg/.hgx/.hx2 files. Starting with Era 3 (NXG), textures are split into standalone .tex files (raw DDS surfaces with 124-byte headers) or bundled into .nxg_textures dictionaries containing a NU20 metadata table followed by concatenated DDS streams.
