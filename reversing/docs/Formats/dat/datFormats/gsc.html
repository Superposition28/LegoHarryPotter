Overview & Engine Evolution

The .gsc (Game Scene / NuScene) family is the primary static environment and background geometry container across Traveller's Tales LEGO titles:

  • Era 1 (LSW1 2005): Stored as .gsc on PS2 (wrapped in RNC\x02 compression), .nux / .nus on Original Xbox (raw GSC0 + VERS chunks), and .nup on PC/GameCube.
  • Era 2 (LSW2 2006 – Indy 1 2008): Stored as .gsc on PS2/PC/Xbox 360 and .nx2 on Original Xbox. Begins with little-endian NU20 or big-endian 02UN chunk streams (INFO, NTBL, TST0, MSH0, MTL0, SPEC).
  • Era 3–4 (NXG & DX11, 2008–2019): Stored with platform suffixes (_nxg.gsc, _wii.gsc, _dx11.gsc). Wrapped in a FourCC resource container (.CC4 / 4CC. + HSER / RESH) embedding an inner NU20 / 02UN scene payload and followed by an out-of-band StreamInfo display-list and vertex/index buffer stream.
