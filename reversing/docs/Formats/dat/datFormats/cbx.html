Overview & Binary Structure (CBX.cs / CBXDecoder)

Verified against TTGamesExplorerRebirthLib/Formats/CBX.cs and TTModding-Docs/src/fileformats/cbx.md: .cbx stands for ChatterBoX, a custom speech and sound-effect compression format derived from Electronic Arts' 10:1 MicroTalk codec:

OffsetSizeTypeFieldDescription
0x004Char[4]Magic!B0X (0x58304221).
0x044Int32VersionFormat version (-2 or -3).
0x084UInt32SampleRateOutput PCM sample rate in Hz (e.g. 24000 or 32000 Hz).
0x0C4UInt32SampleCountTotal decoded 16-bit mono PCM samples (decoded in 0x1B0 = 432-sample frames via SoundExpander16).

Companion .cbs files (and _cbs_ps4.pak in the 2024 PC remaster) store synchronized lip-sync phoneme weights and event triggers.
