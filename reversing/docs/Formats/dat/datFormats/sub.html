Overview

Absent in LSW1, LSW2, and HP 1–4/5–7 (which used pantomime/mumble audio), .sub appears with 2,408 files in LEGO Batman 2 (the first fully voiced TT LEGO title) and grows to 15,826 files in LEGO Dimensions.
