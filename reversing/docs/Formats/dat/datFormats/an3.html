.an3 / .ca3 / .ani / .anm (Skeletal Animation Clip)

Category: Animation · Scope: Universal (.ani in LSW1; .an3 in LSW2–Dimensions; .ca3 on LSW2 X360; .anm in HP/Batman 2)

Format Specification · TT Games Engine

Compressed keyframe skeletal animation tracks storing bone rotation quaternions, translation vectors, and scale curves over time.

Evolution Across TT Games

  • .ani (LSW1 2005): ANI4 / ANI6 keyframe curves; verified byte-identical between PS2 (GAME.DAT) and Original Xbox (chars\amidala\explode.ani = 11,922 B).
  • .an3 (LSW2 2006 – LEGO Dimensions 2015): Introduced in LSW2 (2,440 files in PS2 GAME.DAT and Xbox XISO) and retained through Dimensions (19,580 files) and the 2024 Harry Potter Collection (bundled inside _an3_ps4.pak). Uses 6INA / 8INA / AN3! headers with quantized curve evaluation.
  • .ca3 (LSW2 Xbox 360): Big-endian Xbox 360 animation counterpart mapped in 360_exlist (n3 -> ca3).
  • .anm (HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2): High-precision animation clips used alongside .an3 for complex character and creature rigs.
