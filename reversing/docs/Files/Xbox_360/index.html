Xbox & Xbox 360: Combined File & Directory Layout

Platforms: Original Xbox (XISO XDVDFS at 0x10000) & Xbox 360 (XGD2 XDVDFS at 0x0FD90000 + STFS)

1. Original Xbox & Early Xbox 360 Loose XDVDFS Layout (LSW1 & LSW2)

Uniquely, LEGO Star Wars (2005 Xbox) and LEGO Star Wars II (2006 Xbox & Xbox 360) do not pack their assets into GAME.DAT. All files sit directly inside the XDVDFS partition:

  • LSW1 Xbox (4,721 loose files): default.xbe, chars/ (.hgx, .ani, .bsa), levels/ (.nux, .ter, .rtl, .ai2), cut/ (.cut), sound/ (.wavx).
  • LSW2 Xbox (8,329 loose files): default.xbe, .nx2 scenes, .hx2 models + .hx2.htm Perforce export logs, .an3 animations, .fpk packages.
  • LSW2 Xbox 360 (3,973 loose files + LIVE DLC): default.xex, 360_exlist extension remapping table (ter -> ctr, cu2 -> cc2, an3 -> ca3, vag -> xma), and 57 KB STFS DLC package enabling LSW1 save characters.

2. Xbox 360 PakDat v1.01 Layout (Era 3–4: HP 1–4, HP 5–7, Batman 2)

  • Default.xex: XEX2 Xenon executable container (Title IDs 0x575207E6, 0x57520812, 0x57520817).
  • GAME*.DAT: 57-byte PakDat v1.01 archives storing big-endian PowerPC assets and XMA2 (.xma) audio streams.
