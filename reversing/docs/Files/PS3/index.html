PlayStation (PS2 & PS3): Combined File & Directory Layout

Platforms: Sony PlayStation 2 (ISO-9660 DVD-ROM) & PlayStation 3 (BLES/BLUS Blu-ray Disc)

1. PlayStation 2 Disc Layout (Era 1–2: LSW1 & LSW2)

  • SYSTEM.CNF & SLUS_210.83 / SLUS_214.09: Boot configuration and MIPS R5900 ELF executable.
  • GAME.DAT: Single archive with TOC Version = -1 (4,223 files in LSW1 with RNC\x02 compressed scenes/models; 8,600 files in LSW2).
  • IOP/MODULES/ & FMV/: IOP hardware drivers (.IRX), streaming SPU2 ADPCM audio banks (.MIB/.MIH), and MPEG-2 .PSS movies.

2. PlayStation 3 Blu-ray Layout (Era 3–4: HP 1–4, HP 5–7, LEGO Dimensions)

  • PS3_DISC.SFB & PS3_GAME/PARAM.SFO: Blu-ray disc metadata and Title ID configuration.
  • PS3_GAME/USRDIR/EBOOT.BIN: Signed 64-bit Cell PPU SELF executable.
  • PS3_GAME/USRDIR/GAME*.DAT (and .HDR + INSTALL0_*.DAT in Dimensions): Multi-slice archives (57-byte PakDat v1.01 TOC v-4 with LZ2K in HP; TOC v-7 with DFLT and CMP2 LZMA in Dimensions).

Combined USRDIR Hierarchy

Directory Structure (Combined)

  • USRDIR/EBOOT.BIN (Primary 64-bit PPU executable)
  • USRDIR/audio/ (ATRAC3plus .msf music & cutscene tracks; level_sfx banks)
  • USRDIR/chars/ (character .cd definitions, .ghg models, .an3/.anm animations)
  • USRDIR/cut/ (in-game cutscenes: camera choreography *_ps3.cu2 / .cu3)
  • USRDIR/framework-data/ & gui/ (core engine GUI definitions, .ft2 font atlases)
  • USRDIR/levels/ (story levels and hubs: _nxg.gsc geometry, .dno objects, .led entities, .ter collision)
  • USRDIR/movies/ (high-definition .fmv / .pam Bink cutscenes)
  • USRDIR/shaders/ (compiled RSX .ps3_shaders programs)
  • USRDIR/stuff/ (game.txt parameters, text.csv multilingual dialogue)
