PC (Windows Retail & Steam): Combined File & Directory Layout

Platform: Microsoft Windows (32-bit PE32 Retail/Steam & 64-bit PE32+ AMD64 Remaster)

1. Classic & NXG 32-bit Windows Layout (LSW2 PC & LEGO Batman 2 Steam)

  • LEGO Star Wars II (2006 PC ISO): LEGOSTA2.EXE (7.49 MB, PDB c:\projects\starwars\Release_2K\starwars_pc.pdb) mounting GAME.DAT, PATCH.DAT, and STARTUP.DAT.
  • LEGO Batman 2 (2012 Steam): LEGOBatman2.exe (18.69 MB) mounting GAME0.DAT..GAME6.DAT (27,481 entries, PakDat v1.01 TOC v-4), alongside loose FILETABLE.TXT (18,915 lines), TRACKEDFILELIST360.TXT, ICONS_TEX.PAK (136 files), and Audio/ Ogg/WAV streams.

2. 64-bit Direct3D 11 Remaster Layout (2024 LEGO Harry Potter Collection)

  • harry1.exe / harry2.exe: 64-bit AMD64 executables with shaderblob_pc.blob (13.86 MB) and launcher.pkiwin/.pkdwin.
  • harry1.datwin, harry1b.datwin, harry2.datwin, harry2b.datwin: 64-bit archives (41,389 total files, TOC v-1 with 24-byte entries, 0x12345678 canary, and LZHAM compression).
